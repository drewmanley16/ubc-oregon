Sway prototype

Tap through as Maya the shopper or Jordan the seller. This version follows Drew's gift card system: Jordan hands in a $100 card, Sway moves its balance to a new number, and Maya buys that new card for $88. She's the only one who ever sees its number and PIN. A $3 fee is included in the $88, and one hold on the button pays. The panel shows where you are, what happens behind the scenes, and how many taps it took.

Buttons

Hold to pay is only for the one action that can't be undone. Everything else is a normal tap. Radius 10 inputs, 14 buttons and cards, 22 sheets.

Rules

  • One number gets the spotlight per screen. Everything else hides under "Price details".
  • Green only ever means money you keep (the shopper's savings, the seller's payout).
  • Buttons say exactly what happens and how much: "Hold to pay $88", not "Continue".
  • One honest total. The $3 Sway fee is inside the $88 and shown in details and on the receipt.
  • Errors say what happened, that you weren't charged (only when true), and what to do next.
  • Plain words: "Sign in", "payment record". Arc is named once, in the footer.
  • The card number and PIN appear only for the buyer, only after paying, and never in the public record.
