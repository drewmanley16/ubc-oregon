Design system, v0

One family, Figtree (free on Google Fonts). Extra bold for prices and titles, regular and semibold for everything else.

Buttons and shape

Radius 10 for inputs, 14 for buttons and cards, 22 for sheets. One main button per screen, always at the bottom within thumb reach.

Rules

  • One number gets the spotlight per screen. Everything else hides under "Price details".
  • Green only ever means money the shopper keeps. Never on buttons.
  • Buttons say exactly what happens and how much: "Pay $88", not "Continue".
  • Errors say what happened, that you weren't charged (only when true), and what to do next.
  • No crypto words in the main UI. "Sign in", "Enable payment", "View proof".
